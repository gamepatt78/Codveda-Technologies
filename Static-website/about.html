About us

A small studio with a big greenhouse

Fernhill started with one shelf of cuttings and a habit of giving them away. Today we grow a few dozen varieties and pot each one by hand.

We only sell plants we would keep in our own homes, and we write care notes for each one.

  • Founded: 2019
  • Plants grown: 40+ varieties
  • Care guide: included with every order
